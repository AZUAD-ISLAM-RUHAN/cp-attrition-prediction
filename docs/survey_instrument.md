# Survey instrument (Appendix A of the corrected manuscript)

Google Form "Survey on Predicting the Competitive Programming Journey". Q1–Q6 were shown to everyone. Q6 routed respondents to one of three branches. Response options are as offered on the form, reconstructed from the response sheet; options nobody chose may be missing. n = number of non-empty answers.
